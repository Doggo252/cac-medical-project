# Next version

Ideas that are deliberately out of scope for the competition build. Writing them
down here is how the project says no without losing the idea.

Scope for this version: three letter types, one program (Medi-Cal), two counties
(Santa Clara and San Mateo).

- MC 382, Appointment of Authorized Representative, so a clinic worker or family
  member can act for the user.
- More counties. Each county words its notices a little differently.
- CalFresh and CalWORKs notices, which have their own deadlines and forms.
- Mail and county-portal upload as delivery options alongside fax.
- Reminder notifications before a deadline runs out.
- Languages beyond the four in the plan (English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Chinese).
