# Next version

Ideas deliberately out of scope for the competition build. Writing them down
here is how the project says no without losing the idea.

Scope for this version: three letter types, one program (Medi-Cal), the senior
track (non-MAGI, people 65 and over and people with disabilities), two counties
(Santa Clara and San Mateo). English first, Spanish if on schedule.

Nothing new after October 11 except the agent.

- **MC 216 and MC 217 renewals.** MC 216 is the renewal for families and most
  adults (the MAGI side); MC 217 is for mixed households. Same idea as the
  MC 210 RV, different audience. Both templates are already downloaded in
  `data/templates/`, so this is mostly a generator and classifier extension.
- **Helper mode: MC 382 and MC 380.** Appointment of Authorized Representative,
  so a clinic worker or family member can act for the beneficiary, plus the
  notice that confirms the appointment. Templates already downloaded.
- **Vietnamese and Chinese explanations.** The plan names four languages. Two
  ship this version; the other two wait until a fluent reader can check the
  wording, because an explanation nobody has proofread is worse than none.
- **More counties.** Each county words its notices a little differently.
- **CalFresh and CalWORKs notices**, which have their own deadlines and forms.
- **Mail and county-portal upload** as delivery options alongside fax.
- **Reminder notifications** before a deadline runs out.
